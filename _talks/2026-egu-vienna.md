Oral presentation (EGU26-6933, Session HS3.8) on a data integration methodology for matching and aligning wastewater network objects across multiple, heterogeneous data sources, combining spatial and structural information to produce a unified network representation while resolving conflicts and inconsistencies. Validated on real-world wastewater network data from multiple sources and time periods.

**Authors**: O. Et-Targuy, C. Delenne, S. Benferhat, A. Begdouri
